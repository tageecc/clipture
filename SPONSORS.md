# Clipture supporters / Clipture 赞助名单

Thank you to everyone who helps fund continued maintenance of Clipture.

感谢每一位愿意帮助 Clipture 持续维护的用户。

## Public supporters / 公开赞助者

No names have been published yet. Sponsorships are private unless the supporter explicitly opts in.

目前还没有公开姓名。赞助默认保持匿名，只有用户明确同意后才会展示。

## How listing works / 如何加入名单

After sponsoring, contact [private support](https://clipture.talkape.net/zh/support) from the payment email and provide the public name and optional GitHub or website link you want displayed. We verify the record before updating this file.

赞助后，可以使用付款邮箱通过[私密支持入口](https://clipture.talkape.net/zh/support)提交希望公开的昵称，以及可选的 GitHub 或个人网站链接。核对记录后才会更新此页面。

We never publish payment email addresses, transaction identifiers, exact payment times, or private messages.

我们不会公开付款邮箱、交易编号、精确付款时间或私密沟通内容。
